Project case study

The market still has no unified two-wheeler solution.

While platforms like Porter, MoveMyCar, and Indian Railways Parcel currently exist in the market, there is still a gap for a single, unified solution focused specifically on two-wheelers. Existing services either require manual station drop-offs or lack transparent live tracking. MotoShift solves this by providing an end-to-end service featuring Doorstep Pickup, Digital Pre-Inspection (360° vehicle condition capture), Real-Time GPS Tracking, and Instant Transit Insurance all inside one mobile application.

What we ship

Four pillars. One app.

Built for scooters, commuters, sports bikes, and EVs — not as an afterthought on a car-carrier marketplace.

Doorstep pickup

No railway queues. A verified bike carrier arrives at your gate, scans the booking, and rolls out.

Digital pre-inspection

360° condition capture before the bike leaves — scratches, odometer, and accessories logged in the app.

360° capture in progress

Real-time GPS

Follow the carrier truck, hub scans, and ETA — the transparency railways and many aggregators still skip.

Instant transit insurance

Cover activates the moment pickup is confirmed. No separate call to a logistics desk.

How a shift works

  1. 01

    Book in-app

    Pickup city, drop city, bike type, preferred date. Fare in minutes.

  2. 02

    Inspect at door

    Partner captures a 360° walkaround. You approve the condition report.

  3. 03

    Track the truck

    GPS + hub pings on a dedicated bike carrier, not a mixed household load.

  4. 04

    Doorstep drop

    Receiver verifies the same inspection photos. Insurance stays live until handover.
